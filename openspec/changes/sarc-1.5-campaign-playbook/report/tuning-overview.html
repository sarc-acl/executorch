SARC 1.5 tuning overview

Compared with unmodified ExecuTorch 1.5 (Vulkan backend, no cooperative matrix instructions), 2048-token prefill is 2.8 to 7.9 times faster on the seven GPUs with published figures (geometric mean of six cells: the three models 1B, 3B and 8B, and the two quantization schemes 4w and 8da4w). The large number on each card below is this speed-up; the card's second line splits it into two parts: what the September SARC release had already achieved, and the percentage that this round of tuning added on top of it. Figures 1 to 4 are about the second part: which step the gain over the September release came from, which techniques were used where, where the time was saved, and which work did not pay. For the M51, at the device owner's request, only qualitative conclusions are listed. Figure 8 separately shows how far the tuned kernels of five of the devices are from the hardware roof; its numbers come from the roof study of igpu-roofline.

Where the remaining headroom is

The fused kernel is adopted on six devices, and the M51 uses the two-pass form; the fused-kernel result on the RX 7900 XTX was measured under the AMDVLK driver, with the subgroup width unverified; on 2026-10-09 the owner decided not to re-measure under RADV, and the result is kept as it is. On the B580, the B70 and the Orin attention is down to 5 % to 14 % of prefill dispatch time, and the headroom is in the linear kernels. The roof study of 2026-10-09 (figure 8) shows that the 8da4w kernels of the two Intel cards have only a few percent left within the current structure (an estimated 4 % to 9 % on the B580); the multiply-add stage of the Orin's 8da4w kernel is already at the fed roof at its reuse, and what remains is staging time. For a larger gain the kernel has to perform more matrix multiply-adds for each operand it loads, which is limited by the number of registers (the Orin's 8da4w kernel already uses 209 registers per thread, and 4w uses 128). On 2026-10-09 the owner decided not to start a third round now. On the Orin the fused kernel reaches only 16 % to 20 % of the matrix roof; going faster needs a new kernel that stages K and V through shared memory. On AMD discrete GPUs the HIP backend of llama.cpp has not been measured yet, so the lead holds for Vulkan only.

Process artifacts

A/A calibration and the clock floor, a gate for every candidate, the reference-error rule, the stop rule of two consecutive candidates below 2 %, coordinator holds, independent review. These are part of the playbook and are not in the figures.

Known defect: pipelines lack the full-subgroups flag

The Vulkan specification requires compute pipelines of this kind, which depend on full subgroups, to be created with the "full subgroups" flag. None of the cooperative matrix pipelines on the branches carries it, including the released ones and those of the fused kernels. On 2026-10-09 the owner decided to record this as a known defect and to fix it once, in the release zone, before any merge. The measurements on this page are not affected. The RX 7900 XTX's campaign additionally found that some kernels declare more workgroup shared memory than the limit AMDVLK reports, among them one released 4w kernel; the driver accepts these pipelines and all gates pass. This item will be handled before merging, together with the full-subgroups flag (F1).

Decode slightly slower, cause not investigated

On the B70, with the fused node, decode (generating tokens one by one after prefill) is 0 to 2.5 % slower than the parent configuration; on the B580 all six cells are also slightly below the parent configuration (0.985 to 0.998). The fused kernel does not handle decode calls, and where the slowdown occurs has not been located yet. Decode is not a gate item; it is recorded here so that it is not missed at promotion. The owner decided on 2026-10-09 that the investigation is scheduled before promotion and is not done now.

The Orin's clock

The Orin earlier had two sets of roof figures (matrix fp16 about 16.0 and 9.7 TFLOP/s). The difference comes from the GPU clock: the set of 2026-09-26 was measured with the clock locked at 1020 MHz; the device was rebooted afterwards, and in the 15 W power mode the clock limit is 612 MHz. All Orin figures on this page (tok/s, kernel rates, the roofs of figure 8) are for the 15 W state. The higher power mode was not measured; on 2026-10-09 the owner decided not to measure it.
