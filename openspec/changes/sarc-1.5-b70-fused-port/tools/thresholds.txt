# thresholds.txt: comparison thresholds of sarc-1.5-b70-fused-port (Arc Pro B70, card b70-0). The rules are
# committed before the first measurement; the calibrated values are appended (dated) after the A/A session s1-aa
# and before candidate 1 is timed. Nobody changes a line of this file after a candidate's numbers exist.

valid_run          rc 0; 2048 prompt tokens; 0 generated tokens; no foreign GPU process before, during or after
                   the run (host.sh gpu_others: any DRM client of either B70 that is not ours, or a process whose
                   command line names llama-server, ComfyUI, comfy, ollama, vllm, nvtop, intel_gpu_top, llama_main,
                   test_llama_microbench, Runner.Worker, custom_ops; polled once a second while the runner
                   executes, at least one poll recorded per run); engine time of other DRM clients of the card in
                   the prefill window = 0 (fbusy_pct, sampled every 50 ms; no sample around the window = invalid);
                   >= 5 clock samples in the prefill window (sampler period 10 ms); median GT clock >= CLKMIN; no
                   sample with a thermal throttle reason. An invalid run stays in runs.csv with its reason and is
                   replaced (up to 6 extra pairs a cell).
throttle reasons   what the xe driver exposes on this card: tile0/gt0/freq0/throttle/{status,reasons} and
                   reason_{pl1,pl2,pl4,prochot,ratl,thermal,vr_tdc,vr_thermalert}.
                   Thermal, reject the run: thermal, prochot, ratl, vr_thermalert.
                   Power or current limits, recorded only (throttled_n and the per-sample reasons in the .clk
                   file): pl1, pl2, pl4, vr_tdc. (The first campaign found pl2 asserted in every loaded run.)
foreign job        a foreign GPU process before a run is waited for; after 30 minutes the campaign stops measuring
                   (FOREIGN_STOP) and reports under "Decision needed from the owner".
CLKMIN             97 % of the lowest per-run median clock of the valid runs of s1-aa (one device-wide value)
repeats            5 valid runs per arm and cell; 7 if in s1-aa any cell's A/A ratio is further than 1.0 % from 1
                   or any arm's spread (max - min over its valid runs, relative to its median) exceeds 3.0 %
noise_band         a difference inside +-2 % is noise, not a gain. If the A/A geomean is further than 1 % from 1
                   or any A/A cell is outside +-2 %, nothing is gated until the cause is found
baseline           each cell of the re-measured parent (5617714b0, xe2-refine5) within 3 % of the first
                   campaign's s12-final5: 1B 17964.90 / 20686.90, 3B 7529.41 / 9570.09, 8B 3379.54 / 4481.40 tok/s
                   (4w / 8da4w); a cell outside 3 % is explained before any candidate
kernel_screen      the one screen of task section 5: 3 rounds, cooled before every run, test_llama_microbench
                   --sdpa, kernel time per layer at S = 2048 (fused kernel + copy pass; for the parent QK^T +
                   softmax + attn*V), over the profiles of the B580's selection screen (screen5-select at
                   cea76c634): the parent profile, and the fused variants d64_t32x32s32m8ro, d64_t16x64s16m8g4roj,
                   d64_t16x64s16m8g4oj, d128_t16x64s32m8ro, d128_t16x128s16m8g8oj, d128_t16x64s16m8g4oj. No other
                   variant is screened and none is added.
                   Incumbent per head_dim = the B580's pair (b580-fused1 at cea76c634): d64_t16x64s16m8g4roj and
                   d128_t16x128s16m8g8oj. Another screened variant replaces the incumbent of its head_dim only if
                   its kernel time is at least 3 % below the incumbent's in every one of the 3 rounds for every
                   model of that head_dim (64: 1B; 128: 3B and 8B); among several such, the lowest median.
                   Otherwise b70-fused1 is the B580's pair.
arithmetic (D3)    the fused kernel changes summation order. If a next-token item differs: candidate rms and max
                   error against the fp32 CPU reference not larger than the parent's (xe2-refine5) on every
                   S = 2048 head configuration (tier full), gross-divergence limits 0.5 nat mean KL and one third
                   of the real-text windows (tools/decide.py, unchanged). The reference error is measured and
                   reported in either case. SDPA tiers all / extended / full: 12 passes each, 0 mismatches,
                   pairing=ok, every pass rc 0. Tiers peaked and fused (added with the kernel): 12 passes each,
                   reported, not gate items (as on the B580).
candidate 2        only if the B580 campaign's STATUS.md, read when candidate 1 here is gated, records its
                   candidate 2 (b580-fused2) as accepted with a geomean gain above +2 %; otherwise none.
stop               after candidate 1 (and candidate 2 if it exists), or the end of the budget (one day, 40 USD)
