# sarc-1.5-b580-prefill-refine: 2048-token Llama prefill on the Arc B580, tuned for this card

Device tag `b580`. Branch `topic/b580-prefill-refine`, forked from the head of the B70 campaign
(`topic/xe2-prefill-refine` at `8666b6531`). Parent of every comparison: `6a7cc8cc6` with no profile.
Host: the owner's desktop `fedora` (Fedora 44, Mesa 26.2.3 ANV); the B580 is PCI `0000:03:00.0`, Vulkan
device 0, and drives the display. Artifacts: `/mnt/linux-share/hmz-campaigns/b580/.artifacts/`.

## Why

The B580 and the B70 share every Intel row of the release tables and neither has an SDPA row. The B70 campaign
gained +46.9 % from SDPA kernels alone. The owner wants the two cards tuned separately: the B580 has about
65 % of the B70's matrix roofs and 12 GB instead of 32 GB. Deliverable: the best kernels for this card, and for
every choice whether it equals the B70's and by how much it differs.

## Outcome (2026-10-05; details, tables and evidence paths in `STATUS.md`)

  2048-token prefill **+57.65 % geomean** over the parent `6a7cc8cc6`, per cell 1B 8641 -> 12962 (4w) and
  8828 -> 15170 (8da4w), 3B 3436 -> 5185 and 3519 -> 6400, 8B 1727 -> 2306 and 1845 -> 2999 tok/s
  (session `s6-final`, both arms in one session).
- Gated steps: candidate 0 `b580-refine0` (SDPA QK^T and attn*V kernels) +45.95 %, `ACCEPTED (reference-error
  rule, owner decision 2026-10-04)`, not a plain pass (8B 8da4w next token differs on two prompts);
  candidate 1 `b580-refine1` (8da4w tile `xe2bt_t128x128k64g84s16m8`) +7.67 %, `GATE_PASS`, bit-identical;
  candidate 2 `b580-refine2` (4w band-drain tile) -0.07 %, not adopted; candidate 3 `b580-refine3` (QK^T
  `xe2c` for head_dim 128) +0.16 %, `GATE_PASS`, bit-identical. Candidates 2 and 3 are the two consecutive
  candidates under 2 % that end the campaign.
- **B580 against B70**: every kernel choice is the same on the two cards, with the same rankings; the 8da4w
  tile gains a few points more here (1.31x against 1.26x at kernel level). The two cards compute identical
  values. Table in `STATUS.md`, "Kernel choices for this card, against the B70's".
- Fresh roofs (igpu-roofline `fast`, 2026-10-05): fp16 matrix 112.9 TFLOP/s, int8 matrix 231.4 TOP/s. Final
  linear kernels: 4w 38 to 40 %, 8da4w 28.5 to 29.9 % (from 22 to 23 %).
- Baseline: five cells within +0.4 to +1.9 % of `cells.csv`; 8B 8da4w +9.8 %, outside the 5 % fixed below,
  traced to the old run's disturbed median (two fast and three slow runs on a desktop in use).
- Negative results kept: 17 4w tiles (none faster; a 1.2x reading after one round was a slow base run), 26
  other 8da4w tiles, 57 single-kernel SDPA profiles.

## Thresholds, fixed before any measurement (commit `98e3eccf4` of this file precedes session `s1-aa`; unchanged since)

- A timed run is valid with rc 0, 2048 prompt tokens, 0 generated tokens, no foreign GPU workload, at least
  5 clock samples in the prefill window, no thermal throttle reason (`thermal`, `prochot`, `ratl`) in any
  sample, median GT clock >= `CLKMIN`, foreign engine share in the prefill window <= `BUSYMAX`.
- `CLKMIN` = 97 % of the lowest per-run median clock of the valid runs of the calibration session `s1-aa`
  (one device-wide value). The power-limit flags (`pl1`, `pl2`, `pl4`) are counted, not rejected.
- `BUSYMAX` = max(5 %, 2 x the 90th percentile of the per-run foreign engine share over the valid runs of
  `s1-aa`). The calibration session itself rejects nothing on this criterion; its distribution is reported.
- Repeats: 5 valid runs per arm and cell. 7 if, in `s1-aa`, any cell's A/A ratio is further than 1.0 % from 1
  or any arm's spread (max - min over its valid runs, relative to the median) exceeds 3.0 %.
- Noise band: a difference inside +-2 % is noise. If the A/A geomean is further than 1 % from 1 or any A/A cell
  is outside +-2 %, nothing is optimized until the cause is found.
- Baseline agreement: each cell of the re-measured parent within 5 % of the B580 SARC numbers of
  `sarc-1.5-e2e-benchmark/results/cells.csv` (4w 8605 / 3419 / 1694, 8da4w 8790 / 3507 / 1680 tok/s);
  otherwise stop and find out why.
- Arithmetic changes (every SDPA candidate: fp32 instead of fp16 accumulation) are judged by the owner's
  second decision of 2026-10-04, with `tools/decide.py` unchanged from the B70 campaign: rms and maximum error
  against the fp32 CPU reference not larger than the parent's on the S = 2048 head configurations, all tiers
  12 passes with 0 mismatches, gross-divergence limits 0.5 nat mean KL and one third of the windows.
- Kernel screens choose a kernel per shape only when it is at least 3 % faster than the incumbent at kernel
  level in every round; ties keep the incumbent (and, at equal speed, the B70's choice).
- Done: two consecutive gated candidates each under 2 % geomean over their parent.
