What Changed

baseline:
  dominant shader: linear_vec_buffer_texture2d_half
  activation storage: buffer
  weight storage: texture2d
  path: vector/GEMV style

coopmat:
  dominant shader: linear_coopmat_half
  activation storage: buffer
  weight storage: buffer
  path: KHR cooperative matrix
  tile: 64x64x32

What Did Not Change

CPU fallback aten.cat, output copy cost, elementwise work, and many layout/view conversions stayed roughly the same in absolute time. They now take a larger share because linears got smaller.

The next optimization targets are no longer just "make linear faster": Vulkan KV concat, output-copy path, and layout/view consolidation are now high-value.
