Same Graph, Different Fate

Memory cliff
At larger sequence lengths, activations and fp16 weights exceed the iGPU working set.

S=128 fits comfortably. S=2048 pushes the combined fp16 weights and live activation footprint far beyond the Vulkan-accessible memory budget.

Boundary
Faster matmul shaders do not fix a footprint problem.

To push usable prefill beyond the cliff, the main lever is weight/activation footprint reduction: int4 weights, flash attention, or runtime partitioning/streaming.

Why Prefill Memory Blows Up With seq_len

The model diagram is unchanged across sequence lengths: L=32 dispatches 224 transformer linears, 64 attention BMMs, 2 lm_head matmuls, and the surrounding reshape, norm, softmax, residual, elementwise, and CPU/GPU staging work. What changes is the M dimension of every prefill matmul and the size of the activation tensors.

The attention score tensor is the first term that scales with sequence length squared. It dominates the activation story beyond roughly S=256.

S=128 vs S=2048 Outcome

Dimension S=128 S=2048 Consequence
Linear matmul arithmetic Baseline prefill shapes use M=128. Same shapes with M=2048, so most linears do 16x the work. Expected linear work alone grows roughly with sequence length.
Attention BMM size [1,32,128,128] score tensors. [1,32,2048,2048] score tensors. The attention score term grows 256x and becomes a large activation footprint.
Approximate working set ~16 GB: 15 GB fp16 weights + ~0.9 GB activations. ~34 GB: 15 GB fp16 weights + ~19 GB activations. S=128 fits under the 22.4 GB Vulkan-accessible cap; S=2048 does not.
Observed behavior on 780M Clean measured run. OOM-killed in the measured setup. The failure is driven by memory footprint, not by an unusual graph change.

Bottom Line

S=128 and S=2048 are the same LLaMA 3.1 8B forward graph, but not the same memory problem. The linears grow by 16x in their M dimension, while attention score tensors grow by 256x. On this 780M system, S=128 fits; S=2048 exceeds the practical working-set limit and needs footprint reduction or a different attention/runtime strategy before shader speed matters.
